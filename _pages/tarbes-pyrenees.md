## The Pyrenees near Tarbes

From Tarbes, the mountain range is easily accessible and visible on clear days. The city is a convenient gateway to many remarkable natural sites, including Lourdes, Bagnères-de-Bigorre, the Pic du Midi de Bigorre, Gavarnie, and several scenic valleys.
