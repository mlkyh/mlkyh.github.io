## Academic Profile

**Associate Professor (Maître de conférences), HDR**  
**Materials Science and Tribology**  
Université de Technologie Tarbes Occitanie Pyrénées (UTTOP), France  
Laboratoire Génie de Production (LGP), Tribology, Materials and Processes (TM2P) research group

My research focuses on the experimental investigation of friction, wear, and contact mechanics, with particular emphasis on the physical mechanisms governing the evolution of tribological interfaces.

My work combines in situ and operando experimental techniques, including acoustic emission, high-speed imaging, optical measurements, and Raman spectroscopy, to investigate the dynamics of frictional contacts, third-body behavior, damage mechanisms, and transient lubrication phenomena.

## Research Interests

* **Tribology and contact mechanics**
  * Dry friction, wear, and fretting.
  * Evolution of tribological interfaces and third-body mechanisms.
  * Surface damage and transformations.

* **In situ and operando characterization**
  * Acoustic emission monitoring of frictional contacts.
  * High-speed imaging and optical contact visualization.
  * Raman spectroscopy of tribological interfaces.
  * Multimodal and synchronized experimental measurements.

* **Lubrication and elastohydrodynamics**
  * Transient elastohydrodynamic lubrication (EHL).
  * Reciprocating and oscillating lubricated contacts.
  * Starvation, cavitation, and squeeze-film effects.
  * Analytical and numerical modeling of film thickness evolution.

* **Materials and damage characterization**
  * Ceramics and polymer-based materials.
  * Mechanical behavior and damage monitoring.
  * Indentation and mechanical characterization.
  * Acoustic emission analysis and signal processing.

## Selected Research Projects

* **TriboLumen — In situ investigation of tribological interfaces**
  * Development of experimental approaches combining acoustic emission and optical visualization through semi-transparent contacts.
  * Investigation of friction regimes, contact damage, and acoustic signatures.

* **TriboRaman — Operando Raman characterization of frictional contacts**
  * Coupling of tribological testing and Raman spectroscopy.
  * Investigation of physicochemical transformations within tribological interfaces.

* **ANR NanoLife**
  * Acoustic emission monitoring of damage mechanisms in polymer-based nanocomposites.
  * Investigation of mechanical damage under tensile, cyclic, and indentation loading.

* **DURECOS**
  * Contribution to acoustic emission monitoring and tribological damage characterization.

## Technical and Scientific Expertise

* **Experimental tribology:** reciprocating tribometers, fretting tests, dry and lubricated contacts, friction and wear measurements.
* **Experimental diagnostics:** acoustic emission, high-speed imaging, Raman spectroscopy, optical microscopy, and mechanical testing.
* **Data analysis:** signal processing, statistical analysis, experimental data synchronization, and scientific visualization.
* **Modeling and programming:** Python, Igor Pro, numerical modeling of tribological contacts, and transient EHL calculations.
* **Materials characterization:** indentation, ceramics, polymers, and surface damage analysis.
